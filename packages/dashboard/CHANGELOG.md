## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [`77cbf8a`](https://github.com/kechol/kozo/commit/77cbf8aa890d5cf0a70b7f62f4c0a43667d6cc95) Thanks [@kechol](https://github.com/kechol)! - The dashboard has a board: one column per stage, showing items or tasks. An item's card shows its hold, its work class, its autonomy level and how many of its tasks are complete or held; a task's card shows its hold and revisions. In local mode the board adds items as `kozo add` does, and the arrows on an open item's card move it up or down.
  
  Items now have an order in which kozod starts their work, the order they arrived in until you move one: `kozo reorder <item> [--before <item>]` moves an item before another, or to the end, and appends `item.reordered`. Triage, planning and every task's runs start in item order, and an item's tasks in plan order; a merged task's check still comes first. `kozo ls items` lists items in this order, each open one with the class and level it runs at.

- [`207438d`](https://github.com/kechol/kozo/commit/207438dc43b8d682cd9c8565d8f4f83203dfcc6c) Thanks [@kechol](https://github.com/kechol)! - An item's card on the dashboard's board lists its tasks, each with its goal and its hold, its wait for a merge or its stage, and opens one in the detail pane.

- [`7c69a99`](https://github.com/kechol/kozo/commit/7c69a99332a4ad2c3d96b0d6c14c6943e2fba01c) Thanks [@kechol](https://github.com/kechol)! - The dashboard's detail pane keeps its verbs at its foot, open from the start: what waits, one text field shared by the verbs that take text, and the verbs beside it, with Cmd+Enter or Ctrl+Enter sending the first.

- [`97e4dc7`](https://github.com/kechol/kozo/commit/97e4dc75f2ab3f1cb730e5423d7df369945a905b) Thanks [@kechol](https://github.com/kechol)! - The dashboard shows the detail of an item, a task or a run, reached from the inbox and the board. An item shows its plan, or the draft that waits for your approval with each task's codemod, its tasks, runs and cost. A task shows its DONE_WHEN and scope, its latest run's progress through the blueprint, the run report, the review and verify verdicts with verify's evidence images, every decision log, its diff and its cost. Each shows its events as they happen, and offers the verbs it takes now: send or answer, pause or resume, retry, take it over or give it back, and cancel.
  
  The local API serves the detail view's reads: `GET /api/refs/<task>/diff`, `GET /api/refs/<task>/records` and `GET /api/runs/<run>/evidence/<path>`, which serves only files inside the run's evidence directory, never as a page, and anything but an image or plain text as a download. An item's detail now carries its cost.

- [`cf90681`](https://github.com/kechol/kozo/commit/cf90681101ac6420a2a74abd71cc30f4210e783d) Thanks [@kechol](https://github.com/kechol)! - kozod serves a dashboard at its local address, and `kozo dashboard` opens it in your browser; `kozo dashboard --url` prints the URL instead. The URL carries a code that opens one session, once, within a minute; the page takes it out of the address at once and trades it for a session token for that tab, which lasts while the page uses it and ends after a day unused or when kozod stops, so the API token never reaches the browser. The page shows the factory's status (mode, running or paused, runs, what waits for you and today's cost), with a button that pauses or resumes the factory, and the inbox, oldest first. Each inbox entry offers only the verbs that settle it: approve or cancel a start, approve or reject a plan, answer a question, retry, take over, close or cancel what needs attention. There is no merge button and no bulk approval. The page follows light and dark mode, and its content security policy allows only its own origin.

- [`e0509cf`](https://github.com/kechol/kozo/commit/e0509cf4c484abdfd12e929df33f9ef86a01fcf7) Thanks [@kechol](https://github.com/kechol)! - The dashboard words its labels, verbs, dialogs, empty states, event lines, relative times and dates in the factory's `locale`, and sets the page's language to it.

- [`30d95d1`](https://github.com/kechol/kozo/commit/30d95d18cb62ceac2a301a941c9f744a9376a50b) Thanks [@kechol](https://github.com/kechol)! - The dashboard opens a reference's detail in a pane beside the inbox or the board, or from below on a narrow screen, and the address names both the list and the reference.
