The dashboard's inbox is one list of rows: each leads with what it waits on (start or plan approval, a question, attention, ready to merge), then its title as the link to its detail, its reference and when it began waiting, with the exact time on hover. The verb that settles a row is its one primary button, a row ready to merge links to its diff, and an empty inbox says what will show up there.
