The runs of a task get what the owner said about that task, and about its item from the acceptance of the plan that made the task on. What was said about the item before then shaped the plan and reaches each task through its GOAL and DONE_WHEN, so the review of one task of a split item no longer asks it for the whole item. The text that approves a plan counts as said after its acceptance.
