The dashboard's board uses the window's full width: its columns share it above a minimum, Complete and Cancelled fold to their count until opened, and narrow screens snap column by column. Each card's title is its link, with the whole card as the target, its reference and autonomy level are quiet, an item's task progress shows as a count with a bar, and reordering uses named icon buttons. Items and Tasks switch with a segmented control, and New item opens a dialog with the title, the request, the repository and the work class.
