The dashboard keeps focus where a verb is acted on: opening a verb's field moves focus into it, Back, Escape and a sent action give it back to the verb's button, and Cmd or Ctrl+Enter sends. A sending button says so. Cancel and Close as done first ask in an alert dialog that says what closing ends; when a sent action removes its row, focus moves to the view.
