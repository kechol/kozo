kozod notifies you through the OS when you must act: a task ready for your review and merge, a hold that waits for your approval, answer or attention, the day's spending crossing 50, 80 and 100 percent of the daily budget, the factory pausing on its own, a refused factory definition, and an approval that has waited two, four or six days. macOS shows them in Notification Center through `osascript`, and Linux through `notify-send` when it is installed. Set `KOZO_NOTIFY=off` to turn them off.
