The dashboard's detail view leads with a breadcrumb from item to task to run, the title, and the stage and hold as badges; on wide screens the repository, class, level, scope, cost and runs sit in a side panel. An item or task held for approval offers approving and rejecting as the inbox does, and closed work or a run that ended offers no verbs. The blueprint is a stepper with each node's state in words, the review, verify and findings carry their verdicts as badges, verify's conditions are marked met or not with a glyph, and evidence images sit in a grid. The diff tints added and removed lines and keeps its file headers in view, and the events are grouped by run with their times.
